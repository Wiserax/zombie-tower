DEADWOOD SIEGE — AUDIO 0.3

Original composition and sound design for this project:
Siege bed / siege drums: 32 bars at 88 BPM, D minor. Procedural studio render
from scripts/design-audio.py, with synchronized arrangement and circular delay tails.
Wind, electrical crackle, creature calls, reward chime, cannon pressure/body layers:
original offline DSP renders from the same script.
